# Wayback captures — Irish Sailing's own event pages

Irish Sailing has reorganised `sailing.ie` more than once, and the pages that
carried the All-Ireland / Champions' Cup results are gone from the live site.
These are captures of the Internet Archive's copies, fetched with the `id_`
flag so they are the original bytes rather than the Wayback replay wrapper.

**Read [`../../RESEARCH.md`](../../RESEARCH.md) before using any of these.**
The four year-specific pages say what they are. The three captured from
*undated* URLs do not: they are the live page at a moment in time, and they
carry a forthcoming event's heading over a previous event's results — in one
case with a third year's results still further down. Taking a table off one
of these pages without dating it first is how the wrong sailor becomes a
champion.
