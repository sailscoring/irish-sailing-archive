Irish Sailing All Ireland Sailing Championships 2018

  • VENUE: Lough ReeYacht Club
  • BOATS: SB20
  • DATES: 6 & 7 October 2018
  • FINAL SENIOR TIMELINE: 
  • SENIORS NOTICE OF RACE 2018: HERE
  • SENIORS SAILING INSTRUCTIONS 2018: @ 27 Sept 2018 - updated @ 17:00 HERE

A practice sail is available. Book your slot with DJ Algeo beforehand in LRYC. sailing@lryc.ie

Please note: all sailors will be required to wear bib over your clothing so please ensure that you have a buoyancy aid on you and not an inflatable lifejacket.

There are 16 teams entered in to the Irish Sailing All Ireland Championship. Racing will consist of  –

  1. FLIGHT 1 with 8 competitors
  2. FLIGHT 2 with 8 competitors
  3. REPECHAGE with 8 competitors from 4th, 5th, 6th & 7th place of each flight races
  4. FINAL with top 3 placed from each Flight, plus top 2 placed from REPECHAGE
