Irish Sailing All Ireland Sailing Championships 2019

  • VENUE: National Yacht Club
  • BOATS: Flying Fifteens
  • DATES:  5 & 6 October 2019
  • SENIORS NOTICE OF RACE 2019: HERE
  • SENIORS SAILING INSTRUCTIONS 2019: HERE

 

Please note: all helms will be required to wear bib over your clothing so please ensure that you are wearing a buoyancy aid and not an inflatable lifejacket.

There are 16 teams entered in to the Irish Sailing All Ireland Championship. Racing will consist of  –

  1. FLIGHT 1 with 3 races, 8 teams
  2. FLIGHT 2 with 3 races, 8 teams
  3. REPECHAGE with 2 races, 8 competitors from 4th, 5th, 6th & 7th place of each flight races
  4. FINAL with 4 races, top 3 placed from each Flight, plus top 2 placed from REPECHAGE
