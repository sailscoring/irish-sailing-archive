Irish Sailing All Ireland Junior Sailing Championships 2019

  • VENUE: Fastnet Marine and Outdoor Education Centre
  • BOATS: TR3.6
  • DATES: September 28th and 29th 2019
  • JUNIORS NOTICE OF RACE 2019: HERE
  • ALL IRELAND JUNIORS 2019 SIs : HERE
  • FINAL JUNIOR TIMELINE 2019: see below

There are 16 teams entered in to the Irish Sailing All Ireland Championship. 

The regatta will consist of an opening series of up to 8 races and a Medal Race.

 

Irish Sailing All Ireland Junior Championships 2019 Timeline

Friday 27th of September

 

Practice Sail (2 boats available)

1400-1800hrs. Must be registered. Pre-booking required with Sarah Louise at sl.rossiter@sailing.ie who will issue a timeslot for practice sail.
